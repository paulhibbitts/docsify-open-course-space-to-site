# Module 4 - Select and use effective online instructional approaches

**The course outcome is to:**

*Select and use effective online instructional approaches and align with outcomes.*

The purpose of this module is to continue using the Understanding by Design framework to plan learning experiences and instruction that are aligned with the module learning objectives.

> [!TIP]
> **Learning Objectives**
>
> 1. Select appropriate content and learning activities to ensure students learn what you want them to learn.
> 2. Align the learning experiences and instruction with the module learning objectives.

## [Instructional strategies](module-04-01-instructional-strategies.md) <!-- {docsify-ignore} -->

Instructional strategies are the tools and techniques that Instructors use to design and facilitate learning. They can help students stay motivated, engaged and focused, as well as help them organize information and monitor and assess their own learning.

## [✍Select effective Instructional strategies for online learning](module-04-02-select-effective-instructional-strategies-for-online-learning.md) <!-- {docsify-ignore} -->

In this ITO course, we have started planning our online courses with the end in mind. We have articulated our course outcomes, module learning objectives and decided what types of formative and summative assessments we could use. The final stage of the Backward planning process is deciding how you will teach. In this phase of the design process you need to think about instructional strategies and learning activities that would work best to provide students with the resources and information they need to achieve your course outcomes and objectives.
